# Two minutes, read aloud

Short lines on purpose — easy to read off a phone. **Bold** is what you point
at. Every number is read from the log.

---

### The problem · 20 sec

**Screen: the front page**

Razorpay already moves money between millions of businesses.

So it already knows something nobody else does — **who buys what, from whom,
and at what price.**

That has only ever been used to move a payment from A to B.

Never to introduce A to B.

---

### What we built · 20 sec

**Screen: the network ring**

Every merchant gets an agent. The agents find each other, negotiate, and
settle on real Razorpay payments.

This is a real run. **40 businesses. ₹2 lakh settled between their agents.
41 trading relationships that nobody introduced.**

---

### Watch one happen · 45 sec

**Screen: Morningside — point at the zeroes, then Ask for something**

This business signed up today. Nothing on its books.

*(type: `cold brew concentrate 200 units under 300 each`)*

No form, no supplier list. I say what I need, the way I'd say it.

*(press Find it — switch to the desk tab)*

Same moment, Razorpay's side. Merchant sees plain English. Razorpay sees the
event numbers.

*(switch back as the steps land)*

Found three suppliers. Picked one, and said why.

**Then the gate stopped it** — new supplier, over the cap for a first deal. So
the agent bought a trial quantity instead. **The refusal is written down
either way.**

Paid on a real Razorpay order. Six seconds.

---

### Why a merchant pays · 20 sec

Across the whole run, the agents took **₹11,169 off the asking price** — money
the merchant was going to spend anyway. No procurement hire, no phone calls.

**Screen: switch to Bl Thirdwave**

And this is the paid tier. **Cold brew clears at ₹195 against a ₹210 ask.**
Half of buyers get a discount, so don't pay the ask.

**Screen: switch back to Morningside**

Standard plan. Same card, no numbers — and they were never put in the page.

A seller sees its own prices. A buyer sees its own bills. **Only the company
settling both sides can say what the middle is.**

---

### Close · 15 sec

It also breaks safely. **21 times** Razorpay and our books disagreed — it
caught every one, froze that merchant alone, and repaired it from Razorpay's
own record.

**101 rulings on the log. 35 of them refusals.**

Payments told you a transaction happened.

This tells you who you should be doing business with — and proves every rupee.

## If you only have 60 seconds

Cut *Why a merchant pays* down to the first sentence, and drop the close to
one line: **"Every money action is explainable, bounded and gated — 101
rulings on the log, 35 of them refusals."**

## Don't say

- "fully autonomous" — a person still says yes, and the gate can still refuse
- "saves 8%" — it was **5.5%** across this run; ₹11,169 on ₹202,838
- "replaces procurement" — it negotiates and settles; it doesn't source or
  vet suppliers off-platform
