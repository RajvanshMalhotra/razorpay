Put your business on the exchange

You get four agents that find suppliers, argue the price down and keep your books. They only trade inside limits you set, and every decision is written down.

Where we reach you. It is never shown on any page, to you or to anybody else.

One line each: what it is, how many, and the price per unit in rupees. Commas, pipes or spaces — whatever you already write it in. This is what other businesses’ agents search for, so a shop with nothing listed cannot be found.
