CAPTURE SETTINGS

Record at 1600x1000 or wider. Below about 1000px wide the network
ring and the desk collapse to the mobile layout and lose the density
that makes the point.

Let animations finish before cutting. The highlighter takes about one
second, the negotiation replays over about twelve, the ring tours
every 1.15 seconds. Cutting mid-motion reads as a glitch.

Leave the passcode screen visible for a beat in Clip 6. It says out
loud that the gate is a stage prop and not access control. A judge
noticing that you were honest about it is worth the second it costs.

If a number on screen disagrees with the voiceover, the screen is
right. Regenerate the pages and correct the narration.
