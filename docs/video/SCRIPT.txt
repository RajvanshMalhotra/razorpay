HOW TO READ THIS

  [ANIM]   generate in Runway / Kling / Veo. Prompt is in
           docs/video/prompts/shot-N.txt (shot-N-veo.txt for Veo).
  [SCREEN] record yourself off the real product. Cannot be
           generated, and that is the point of it.
  [CARD]   black type on plain white, made in the editor. The
           video models render letters as garbage, so every word
           on screen is cut in afterwards.
  [VO]     narration. Numbers are written as they are read aloud.

Every number spoken is read from the run log and is correct as
written. If a number on screen ever disagrees with the narration,
the screen is right - regenerate the pages and fix the narration.

Beats marked >> CUT FIRST << are what to drop for a 3:00 version.
Chapters 3 and 4 are the graded bar. Never cut those.

==================================================================
CAPTURE SETTINGS
==================================================================

Record at 1600x1000 or wider. Below about 1000 pixels wide the
network ring and the desk collapse to the mobile layout and lose
the density that makes the point.

Let animations finish before cutting. The highlighter takes about
one second. The negotiation replays over about twelve. The ring
tours every 1.15 seconds. Cutting mid-motion reads as a glitch.

A thin strip along the bottom of every screen recording, small
mono type:

  read from the event log . nothing on this screen was typed by hand

So that no frame of real product is ever unlabelled.
