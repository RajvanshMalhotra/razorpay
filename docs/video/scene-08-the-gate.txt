SCENE 8 - REFUSED, THEN A SMALLER FIRST TRADE
One 15 second clip. Events 257 to 261, in order.

Every beat below is a real logged event. The numbers are exact.


VISUAL

Continue directly from Scene 7. Two stick figures, one on each
side of a low table. Between them stands a third figure with one
arm held straight out: the gate.

On the table in front of the Trader is a TALL STACK of small
yellow coins. Pinned above the stack, a small hand-drawn tag:

  195 / unit

That tag never changes for the whole scene. This is the point of
the scene.

The Trader slides the tall stack toward the gate.

The gate's arm stays out. A hand-drawn stamp lands hard over the
stack:

  DENIED

Beat. Hold on it. The Trader does not flinch and does not walk
away. It puts one hand under its chin.

A small thought bubble appears above it:

  Smaller first.

The Trader lifts most of the coins off the stack and sets them
aside, keeping a short stack. The tag above it does not move. It
still reads 195 / unit.

The Trader slides the SHORT stack toward the gate.

A second stamp lands, next to the first:

  APPROVED

The arm lowers, slowly. The Trader pushes the short stack under
the lowered arm and across the table. The seller takes it.


ON SCREEN

  Left of frame, appearing with the first stamp:
    258 - DENIED - 31,200 over the 20,000 cap

  Left of frame, appearing with the second stamp:
    260 - APPROVED - 4,875

  Above the stack throughout, never changing:
    195 / unit


VOICEOVER

  "A hundred and sixty units came to thirty-one thousand two
  hundred rupees. Over the twenty thousand rupee cap on any
  single payment. So the gate refused it."

  (on the think beat)

  "The agent did not drop its price. It came back for twenty-five
  units instead of a hundred and sixty, at the same one hundred
  and ninety-five."

  (as the short stack goes through)

  "Four thousand eight hundred and seventy-five rupees. Allowed,
  and paid, on a real Razorpay order."


FINAL FRAME

The two figures complete the exchange. The gate's arm is down
behind them. The DENIED and APPROVED stamps are both still
visible, side by side.

Large handwritten text:

  BOTH RULINGS ARE THE RECORD

Beat. Then underneath, smaller:

  a refusal is not a failure


ANIMATION DETAIL

The whole scene is: TRY - BLOCKED - THINK - SHRINK - RETRY -
APPROVED. Let the THINK beat breathe. It is the only moment in
the film where an agent visibly changes its mind.

Two things must be unmistakable:

  1. The price tag never moves. What shrinks is how much is
     being bought, not what is being paid per unit. The agent
     conceded nothing.

  2. Both stamps stay on screen at the end. The gate ruling on
     the yes is a recorded event too, not a transition. That is
     the whole argument of the film.

Do not animate coins flying or sparkling. The stack is lifted by
hand and set down by hand.
