AGENT EXCHANGE - ON-SCREEN TEXT CARDS
Made in the editor, not by the video model. Black type on plain
white, same hand-drawn feel as the animation. Warm yellow only on
the underline or the number.

These carry the film on their own. If the voiceover is not in the
cut yet, these cards are the only thing telling a viewer what this
is - so they are not optional.
==================================================================

THE SIX CHAPTER CARDS
Each one is a hard cut, full frame, held 2 seconds, immediately
before its screen recording. The number is warm yellow, the words
are black. This is the spine - a judge can track exactly where
they are in the film and what they are about to be shown.

  1    It finds who to deal with          (before the network ring)
  2    It argues the price                (before the offers scroll)
  3    Every rupee is ruled on first      (before the rail)
  4    It catches its own mistakes        (before the freeze/repair)
  5    The books keep themselves          (before the Google Sheet)
  6    What only Razorpay can see         (before the desk)
