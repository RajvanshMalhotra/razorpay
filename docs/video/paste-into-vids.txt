THE AGENT EXCHANGE - VIDEO SCRIPT
Eighteen scenes. Each one is a single 15 second clip: one action,
about thirty words. Nothing is meant to happen fast.

Style for every scene: 2D hand-drawn stick figure animation on a
plain white background. Black ink lines with uneven hand-drawn
weight. Simple round heads, dot eyes, no faces. Flat, no shading,
no gradients, no 3D. One accent colour only, a warm yellow.
Locked-off camera, no zoom. Movements are slow and deliberate,
with long pauses. Calm, unhurried, confident.


SCENE 1

VISUAL: One stick figure stands alone behind a small counter. It
slowly looks left. Long pause. It slowly looks right. Nobody
comes. It shrugs. Hold on the still frame.

ON SCREEN: Finding suppliers takes time nobody has

NARRATION: Every business needs suppliers, and needs buyers.
Finding them, and checking whether they are real, takes time a
small business does not have.


SCENE 2

VISUAL: Wide shot of forty tiny stick figures, each alone behind
its own small counter, evenly spaced across the frame. None of
them face each other. They stay completely still for the whole
clip except for one slow blink.

ON SCREEN: So most trade with whoever they already know

NARRATION: So most of them trade with whoever they already know,
at whatever price happens to be listed. Nobody has time to look
any further than that.


SCENE 3

VISUAL: The same grid of forty figures. One single warm yellow
line draws itself slowly from one figure to another across the
frame. Nothing else moves. Hold.

ON SCREEN: Razorpay already sits between all of them

NARRATION: But Razorpay already sits between all of them. It
already knows who is real. It already knows who actually pays.


SCENE 4

VISUAL: The same grid. More warm yellow lines draw themselves on,
one after another, slowly, until a web connects most of the
figures. The figures stay still.

ON SCREEN: THE AGENT EXCHANGE

NARRATION: So we built the Agent Exchange. Every merchant gets an
agent. It finds who to deal with on that network, argues the
price down, and settles on real Razorpay payments.


SCENE 5

VISUAL: A stick figure offers a small yellow parcel to another
stick figure. A third figure stands between them with one arm
held straight out like a barrier. The parcel stops at the arm.
Long hold. Nothing else happens.

ON SCREEN: Every rupee is ruled on before it moves

NARRATION: And every rupee it moves is checked against a limit
you set, ruled on before it moves, and written to a log you can
read back afterwards.


SCENE 6

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING of the exchange landing page. Do not
generate this.

ON SCREEN: nothing here was typed by hand

NARRATION: This is a real run. Thirty-two businesses. Nine
hundred and ninety-one recorded events. Not one figure you are
about to see was typed by hand.


SCENE 7

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING of a ring of merchant names with lines
between the ones that traded. Do not generate this.

ON SCREEN: 1 - It finds who to deal with

NARRATION: Thirty-two businesses, and thirty-six trading
relationships between them. Nobody made a single introduction.
The agents found each other.


SCENE 8

VISUAL: Two stick figures face each other in profile, one on each
side of the frame. An empty speech bubble pops above the left
one. Pause. An empty bubble pops above the right one. Pause. Two
more, slowly, each smaller than the last.

ON SCREEN: 2 - It argues the price
In the bubbles as they pop: 200, 205, 190, 195

NARRATION: This is what that looks like. Two agents, arguing
about price, in plain English, until one of them gives way.


SCENE 9

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING of the real quoted offers. Do not
generate this.

ON SCREEN: their actual words, quoted from the log

NARRATION: And these are their actual words, quoted from the log.
Five rounds, closing from two hundred rupees a unit down to a
hundred and eighty-five.


SCENE 10

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING of the audit trail, first two stations.
Do not generate this.

ON SCREEN: 3 - Every rupee is ruled on first

NARRATION: Every step carries the number of the event it came
from. Event two hundred and five. The merchant wants a hundred
and sixty units of cold brew.


SCENE 11

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING of the audit trail, next two stations.
Do not generate this.

ON SCREEN: event 217 - event 256

NARRATION: Event two hundred and seventeen. Its agent shortlisted
three sellers and argued for one. Event two hundred and
fifty-six. Agreed at a hundred and ninety-five rupees a unit.


SCENE 12

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING of the refusal on the audit trail. Rest
on it. Do not generate this.

ON SCREEN: event 258 - REFUSED

NARRATION: Event two hundred and fifty-eight, and here is the
part that matters. The gate refused it. Thirty-one thousand two
hundred rupees was over the twenty thousand rupee cap on any one
payment.


SCENE 13

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING, still on the refusal, then the smaller
retry, then the payment. Do not generate this.

ON SCREEN: the ruling is recorded, not just the outcome

NARRATION: So the agent came back for twenty-five units instead
of a hundred and sixty, at the same price. Four thousand eight
hundred and seventy-five rupees. Allowed, and paid.


SCENE 14

VISUAL: A stick figure holds an open ledger. Beside it a second
figure holds an identical ledger with different scribbles on it.
The first figure notices, and freezes mid-step with one foot
raised. Long hold on the frozen figure.

ON SCREEN: 4 - It catches its own mistakes

NARRATION: Then it broke. Event seven hundred and sixty-three.
Razorpay said that payment was captured. Our books said pending.
Nobody was watching.


SCENE 15

VISUAL: A third stick figure walks in slowly, takes both ledgers,
makes a single mark, and hands them back matching. The frozen
figure puts its foot down and walks on.

ON SCREEN: froze one merchant, not the market

NARRATION: The accountant found it on a routine sweep. It froze
that one merchant, not the whole market, and repaired the
settlement using the payment id Razorpay gave back.


SCENE 16

VISUAL: SCREEN RECORDING of the repair and the resume on the
audit trail. Do not generate this.

ON SCREEN: 17 mismatches caught - 0 humans

NARRATION: Then it let that merchant trade again. A freeze that
never lifts is a ban, not a hold. Seventeen mismatches were
caught this way. No human touched any of them.


SCENE 17

VISUAL: The frame is split by a thin vertical line. On the left a
stick figure lies asleep, completely still for the whole clip. On
the right the same figure, drawn in warm yellow, slowly walks
across and hands a small parcel to another figure.

ON SCREEN: 5 - The books keep themselves

NARRATION: You can only be in one meeting at a time. Your agent
cannot. The books keep themselves, into the merchant's own Google
Sheet, while you are asleep.


SCENE 18

VISUAL: Many small stick figures along the bottom of the frame,
each looking down at its own tiny counter. High above them, one
figure stands on a raised horizontal line and looks slowly across
all of them. Yellow lines connect the figures below.

ON SCREEN: 6 - What only Razorpay can see

NARRATION: And the processor sees what no single merchant can.
Which campaigns are climbing across the whole client base, sold
on by sealed auction, with a royalty paid back.


SCENE 19

VISUAL: The full grid of forty stick figures, densely connected
by steady warm yellow lines. Every figure turns to face the
centre together in one slow motion. Long hold, then fade to
white.

ON SCREEN: 991 events - 74 rulings - 17 repairs - 0 humans

NARRATION: Payments told you a transaction happened. This tells
you who you should be doing business with. And it proves every
rupee of it.
