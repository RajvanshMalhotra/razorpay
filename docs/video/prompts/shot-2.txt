2D hand-drawn stick figure animation. Plain white background. Black ink
lines, uneven hand-drawn weight, slight boil between frames. Simple round
heads, no faces except dot eyes. Flat, no shading, no gradient, no 3D. One
accent colour only: warm yellow. Locked-off camera, no zoom, no parallax.
12 frames per second. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no
logos. A grid of forty lone stick figures. A single warm yellow line draws
itself from one figure to another across the frame. Then a second line,
then several more, until a sparse web of yellow lines connects most of
them. The lines draw on progressively. They do not fade in. The figures
stay still.
